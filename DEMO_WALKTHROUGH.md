# Presentation script

“ClassPulse allows educational-purpose discussions using approved college accounts. Students verify their listed email and choose a ClassPulse password. Their identity and section come from the college roster.”

1. Show Student activation for student1@college.example, the roster profile and the clearly labelled Demo Email Inbox. Complete activation with a password of your choice.
2. Show that teacher@college.example cannot activate before approval.
3. Sign in as admin@college.example / AdminDemo123!, edit the teacher invitation and approve Java,DAA in section L.
4. Activate the teacher email, then show its pending questions. The section M Java question is absent.
5. Resolve the Java garbage-collection question with an academic answer, publish it and browse the public FAQ.
6. As administrator, change the teacher assignment to M. Refresh its dashboard to show section M questions. Revoke approval, then refresh a protected teacher screen to demonstrate loss of access.
7. As a student, submit “How was your weekend?” to demonstrate rejection, and a specific Java question to demonstrate acceptance.

“Email delivery here is simulated for the prototype. A real deployment would connect the college email service or college SSO, along with its approved roster. The message classifier uses English rules and still has relevance limitations.”
